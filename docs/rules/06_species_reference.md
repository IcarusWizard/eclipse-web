# 06. Species & Factions Reference

---

## 1. Human (Terran) Factions

All 6 Terran factions share standard human ship blueprints, base action activations, and starting resources, differentiated by their home sectors and background:

### Standard Terran Baseline
- **Starting Resources**: 3 Money, 2 Science, 4 Materials.
- **Starting Discs**: 13 Influence Discs.
- **Starting Colony Ships**: 3 Colony Ships (all ready).
- **Trade Ratio**: **2:1** (2 of any resource for 1 of another).
- **Activations per Action**:
  - Explore: **1**
  - Research: **1**
  - Upgrade: **2**
  - Build: **2**
  - Move: **3** (Humans receive +1 base Move activation over standard aliens)
  - Influence: **2**

### Terran Faction Sectors
| Faction | Home Sector Number | Sector Characteristics |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Terran Directorate** | Sector 221 | 3 Standard habitats (1 Money, 1 Science, 1 Material) + 2 Advanced habitats. |
| **Terran Federation** | Sector 223 | Standard Terran setup with balanced starting economy. |
| **Terran Republic** | Sector 225 | Standard Terran setup. |
| **Terran Union** | Sector 222 | Standard Terran setup. |
| **Terran Conglomerate** | Sector 224 | Standard Terran setup. |
| **Terran Alliance** | Sector 226 | Standard Terran setup. |

## 2. Alien Species Overview

The alien species feature asymmetrical starting setups, unique blueprints, special abilities, and varied action activations:

### 1. Eridani Empire
- **Lore**: Ancient fallen empire with superior starting technology but decaying financial reserves.
- **Starting Resources**: 26 Money, 2 Science, 4 Materials.
- **Starting Colony Ships**: 3
- **Starting Techs**: `gauss_shield`, `fusion_drive`, `plasma_cannon`.
- **Special Setup**: Starts with **2 random Reputation Tiles** drawn facedown onto their track.
- **Trade Ratio**: 2:1.
- **Activations**: Standard (Explore 1, Research 1, Upgrade 2, Build 2, Move 2, Influence 2).

### 2. Hegemony of Nia
- **Lore**: Aggressive, warlike conquerors specialized in military domination and heavy combat.
- **Starting Resources**: 3 Money, 2 Science, 4 Materials.
- **Starting Techs**: `improved_hull`, `plasma_cannon`.
- **Trade Ratio**: 2:1.
- **Starting Blueprint**: Dreadnoughts start equipped with heavy armor and cannon configurations.

### 3. Planta
- **Lore**: Sentient plant collective that spreads rapidly across the galaxy without engaging heavily in fleet expansion.
- **Starting Resources**: 3 Money, 2 Science, 4 Materials.
- **Starting Colony Ships**: **4 Colony Ships** (starts with 1 extra).
- **Special Abilities**:
  - **Rapid Spread**: Explore action grants **2 Explore Activations** (instead of 1).
  - **Photosynthesis VP**: At game end, scores **+1 bonus Victory Point per controlled sector**.
  - **Weak Blueprints**: Blueprints have fewer slots and ships cannot equip heavy weaponry or heavy hulls.
- **Trade Ratio**: **3:1** (weaker trading economy).

### 4. Descendants of Draco
- **Lore**: Mysterious species with ancestral ties to the Ancients.
- **Starting Resources**: 3 Money, 2 Science, 4 Materials.
- **Special Abilities**:
  - **Ancient Kinship**: Can explore sectors containing Ancients without being attacked immediately; Ancients do not pin Draco ships.
  - Can colonize and place influence discs in sectors guarded by Ancients.
  - Ancient ships defend against opponent attacks alongside Draco ships.
- **Trade Ratio**: 2:1.

### 5. Mechanema
- **Lore**: Cybernetic synthetic intelligence focused on industrial automation and cheap ship construction.
- **Starting Resources**: 3 Money, 2 Science, 4 Materials.
- **Starting Techs**: `positron_computer`.
- **Special Abilities**:
  - **Automated Production**: Ship build costs are **discounted by 1 Material**:
    - Interceptor: **2 Materials** (instead of 3)
    - Cruiser: **4 Materials** (instead of 5)
    - Dreadnought: **7 Materials** (instead of 8)
    - Starbase: **2 Materials** (instead of 3)
  - **High Activations**: Upgrade action grants **3 Upgrade Activations**; Build action grants **3 Build Activations**.
- **Trade Ratio**: 2:1.

### 6. Orion Hegemony
- **Lore**: Noble martial race with advanced defensive shielding and starbase engineering.
- **Starting Resources**: 3 Money, 2 Science, 4 Materials.
- **Starting Techs**: `gauss_shield`, `neutron_bombs`.
- **Starting Blueprints**: Starts with reinforced shields pre-printed on all ship blueprints.
- **Trade Ratio**: 2:1.
